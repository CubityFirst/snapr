# snapr brand exploration

This folder holds the chosen brand direction, **Focus Field**: precise,
professional, and closest to the current product. Open `index.html` in a
browser to see it.

The wordmark is a working-name placeholder. As of October 2026, a separate
product named **Snapr** is already active in the same screenshot and screen
recording category:

- <https://snapr.pineple.com/>
- <https://www.producthunt.com/products/snapr-5>

There is also a larger photography and visual-content company named **Snappr**:

- <https://www.snappr.com/>

That makes independent trademark, company-name, package-name, domain, and app
store clearance a priority before a final identity is commissioned or launched.
This exploration is design strategy, not legal clearance.

## Why Focus Field

The codebase already uses the blue `#3D9BFF` viewfinder mark and accent, so it
costs almost nothing to adopt. Its weak spot is distinctiveness, which the
voice and copy have to carry.

## Included files

- `index.html` — the Focus Field brand sheet
- `assets/focus-field-mark.svg` — vector concept mark
- `assets/focus-field.png` — generated atmosphere art
- `IMAGE_PROMPTS.md` — the exact image-generation prompts

The SVG mark is a deliberately simple concept asset. It has not yet gone
through optical correction, small-size testing, trademark clearance, or a full
export pass.

## Suggested final packet

- Primary, horizontal, stacked, monochrome, and reversed logos
- App icons from 16 px through 1024 px, plus Windows `.ico` and macOS `.icns`
- SVG, PDF, and transparent PNG exports
- Colour tokens with light/dark UI roles and accessible text pairings
- Typography files or links, licences, and usage rules
- Screenshot frames, annotation motifs, and background templates
- Social avatar, social card, repository banner, and launch graphics
- One-page press sheet and a concise voice-and-copy guide
